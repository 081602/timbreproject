On March 8, 1995 the courts decided that IBM was not responsible for repetitive-stress injuries suffered by some computer users. Plaintiffs had argued that IBM should have provided warnings that overuse of keyboards could cause injuries. To celebrate the anniversary of this historic event, the Timbre Project has agreed to lure people away from their keyboards by playing music this Saturday night at Toad in Porter Sq, Cambridge.

To make it even more appealing, the Timbre Project will be playing a Rolling Stones album from front to back. The album is Some Girls - so don't miss it.
