Tony Randall would pass Jaime on the streets of NY as Tony would go pick up his daily dose of Haagen Dazs ice cream (1977). Tony Randall was in The King of Comedy (1983) with Tony Devon. Tony Devon was in Telling Lies in America (1997) with Kevin Bacon.

So, with a Bacon number of 3, The Timbre Project's Jaime is excited to be playing tomorrow night at TT The Bear's on Kevin's birthday. It is rare that one can celebrate such an occasion at such an excellent venue. We hope you'll join the celebration!

As an added bonus, it's movie theme night at TT's, and The Timbre Project will be playing a few cuts from Harold & Maude while the movie is projected on stage or perhaps even on the band themselves. Hmmm.....this could get interesting.
